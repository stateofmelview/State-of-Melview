⚖️ 4-Phase Trial Sequence & Standards

  1. Phase I: Arraignment & Plea: Formal reading of charges; defendant enters plea.
  2. Phase II: Evidentiary Submission: Probative evidence and witness records registered.
  3. Phase III: Oral Arguments: State and Defense present closing statements.
  4. Phase IV: Verdict & Sentencing: Judge issues binding written verdict and sentence.

* State Bar certification requires a verified 80% passing threshold. Courtroom contempt is subject to ejection under § 8-405.

🚔 Police Pursuit & PIT Guidelines

  • Code 3 Authorization: Emergency lights and sirens required during active vehicular pursuit.
  • Strict PIT Restriction: Precision Immobilization Technique (PIT) is strictly prohibited at speeds exceeding 35 MPH.
  • Custodial Transfer: Suspect must be formally processed within 15 minutes of scene clearance.
  • Maximum Confinement: Absolute 30-minute custodial cap per § 8-103.

Constitution of the State of Melview

CONSTITUTION OF THE STATE OF MELVIEW

Adopted 25 August 2026 • Supreme Governing Instrument
Drafted by jackbillyeye • Government Oversight

PREAMBLE

We, the people and members of the State of Melview, in order to establish an orderly, realistic, accountable, and playable State; to preserve liberty while maintaining public order; to divide governmental power so that no single role may exercise all authority; to provide fair elections and dependable institutions; to secure due process and equal treatment; to recognize the practical authority of the community owners, developers, and administrators over the Roblox group and game; and to promote professional roleplay, public service, and responsible leadership, do hereby establish this Constitution as the supreme governing instrument of the State of Melview roleplay government.

ARTICLE 1 - DECLARATION OF RIGHTS AND LIBERTIES

Section 1. Inherent Dignity and Equality: All residents are entitled to equal treatment under the law. No governmental officer may deny a roleplay service, hearing, employment opportunity, or public benefit on arbitrary or discriminatory grounds.

Section 2. Freedom of Expression: Residents may criticize officers, departments, laws, and policies in good faith, subject to rules against harassment, threats, spam, impersonation, or disruption of official operations.

Section 3. Peaceful Assembly: Residents may organize demonstrations, meetings, political campaigns, and civic events, provided they comply with lawful time, place, safety, and game-session rules.

Section 4. Due Process: No resident shall be deprived of a roleplay license, public position, or governmental privilege for alleged misconduct without notice of the allegation and a reasonable opportunity to respond, except for temporary emergency measures necessary for immediate safety.

Section 5. Presumption of Innocence: A person accused of a roleplay offense is presumed innocent until the competent authority makes a finding under applicable law.

Section 6. Search and Seizure: Government agents shall not conduct unreasonable searches of roleplay persons, vehicles, or property. Warrants or recognized exceptions shall be required when the applicable code demands them.

Section 7. Counsel and Representation: A person facing a serious roleplay criminal charge may request representation by an attorney or eligible public defender when the legal system provides such a service.

Section 8. Privacy and Communications: Government personnel shall not misuse private community information, leaked logs, or administrative data for roleplay advantage. Confidential records shall be handled under community-management rules.

Section 9. Right to Petition: Residents may file complaints, requests, petitions, legislative proposals, and appeals to competent officials without retaliation for making the complaint in good faith.

Section 10. Right to Vote: Qualified citizens have the right to participate in elections, referenda, and recalls administered under this Constitution, subject to eligibility rules and platform limitations.

Section 11. Protection from Arbitrary Punishment: No officer shall impose a penalty solely because a resident is unpopular, disagrees with leadership, or declines an improper order. Government action must have a constitutional, statutory, departmental, or valid emergency basis.

Section 12. Right to a Fair Hearing: Courts and administrative bodies shall provide a neutral decision-maker, notice of the issue, an opportunity to present relevant information, and a written or recorded decision where practicable.

ARTICLE 2 - SOVEREIGNTY, CITIZENSHIP, AND GOVERNMENT STRUCTURE

Section 1. State Status: Melview shall be known officially as the State of Melview. Government authority is exercised for the benefit of the roleplay community and its residents.

Section 2. Source of Government: All governmental roleplay authority ultimately derives from the community members participating in the State, subject to the reserved authority of the community owners, developers, and administrators recognized in Article III.

Section 3. Three Branches: The roleplay government shall be divided into legislative, executive, and judicial branches. No officer shall simultaneously exercise incompatible powers of two branches.

Section 4. Legislative Branch: Legislative power is vested in the General Assembly, operating primarily through the State Senate.

Section 5. Executive Branch: Executive power is vested in the Governor, Lieutenant Governor, and executive departments established by law.

Section 6. Judicial Branch: Judicial power is vested in the Supreme Court of Melview and competent lower courts.

Section 7. Supremacy: This Constitution is supreme within the roleplay government. Any conflicting statute or executive order is void.

ARTICLE 3 - COMMUNITY MANAGEMENT AND DEVELOPER AUTHORITY

Section 1. Reserved Community Authority: The owner, developer, and management body constitute the Community Management Authority.

Section 2. Platform Powers: Community Management retains exclusive authority over group permissions, server infrastructure, assets, anti-exploit measures, and Roblox platform compliance.

Section 3. Autonomy: Ordinary roleplay governance is carried out independently by state officials within constitutional limits.

ARTICLE 4 - THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY

Section 1. Composition: The General Assembly operates through a Senate composed of 5 to 9 Senators.

Section 2. Veto & Review: The Governor has 5 days to sign or veto bills. The Senate may override a veto by a two-thirds (2/3) vote.

ARTICLE 5 - THE GOVERNOR AND LIEUTENANT GOVERNOR

Section 1. Chief Executive: The Governor executes laws, commands the State Guard, and issues lawful administrative orders.

Section 2. Election & Term: Elected jointly for a 4-month term, with a maximum of two consecutive terms.

ARTICLE 6 - EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENTS AND CABINET

Provides for the Cabinet, Secretary of the State, principal department heads, and administrative rulemaking.

ARTICLE 7 - DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE & PUBLIC LEGAL SERVICE

Establishes the Attorney General, State Bar (80% score threshold), Public Defender's Office, and law enforcement training standards.

ARTICLE 8 - PUBLIC SAFETY, EMERGENCY SERVICES, & STATE GUARD

Establishes the Melview State Police, local Sheriff offices, fire-rescue services, and State Guard activation limits.

ARTICLE 9 - THE JUDICIARY

Establishes the Supreme Court (Chief Justice and Associate Justices), District Courts, judicial review, and strict warrant particularity.

ARTICLE 10 - ELECTIONS & ELECTORAL COMMISSION

Independent 3-member commission, 14-day anti-alt voter checks, ranked-choice voting, and recall referenda (60% threshold).

ARTICLES 11 THROUGH 17 - LOCAL GOVERNANCE, ETHICS, EMERGENCY POWERS & AMENDMENTS

Covers municipal authority (Art. 11), civil service & oaths (Art. 12), impeachment by 2/3 vote (Art. 13), 14-day emergency declarations (Art. 14), 72-hr FOIA transparency (Art. 15), amendment referenda (Art. 16), and transitional rules (Art. 17).

Constitutional Supremacy & Rule of Law:

Pursuant to Article II, Section 7, the Constitution is the supreme law of the State of Melview. All peace officers, magistrates, and executive officers are strictly bound by the statutory 30-minute maximum custodial limit under Title 8 (§ 8-103), warrant particularity under Title 7, and public transparency mandates under Article XV.

§ 401. Law Enforcement Pursuit Protocol. PIT maneuvers are restricted to under 35 MPH unless deadly force is justified.

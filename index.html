A practical state constitution for an organized roleplay government, adapted from Melview constitutional principles, United States constitutional structure, and established RO-state governance models[cite: 1].

PREAMBLE

We, the people and members of the State of Melview, in order to establish an orderly, realistic, accountable, and playable State; to preserve liberty while maintaining public order; to divide governmental power so that no single role may exercise all authority; to provide fair elections and dependable institutions; to secure due process and equal treatment; to recognize the practical authority of the community owners, developers, and administrators over the Roblox group and game; and to promote professional roleplay, public service, and responsible leadership, do hereby establish this Constitution as the supreme governing instrument of the State of Melview roleplay government[cite: 1].

Section 1. INHERENT DIGNITY AND EQUALITY: All residents are entitled to equal treatment under the law[cite: 1].

Section 2. FREEDOM OF EXPRESSION: Residents may criticize officers, departments, laws, and policies in good faith[cite: 1].

Section 3. PEACEFUL ASSEMBLY: Residents may organize demonstrations and meetings complying with safety rules[cite: 1].

Section 4. DUE PROCESS: No resident shall be deprived of a roleplay license or public position without notice and an opportunity to respond[cite: 1].

Section 5. PRESUMPTION OF INNOCENCE: A person accused of a roleplay offense is presumed innocent until proven guilty[cite: 1].

Section 6. SEARCH AND SEIZURE: Government agents shall not conduct unreasonable searches without particularized warrants[cite: 1].

Section 7. COUNSEL AND REPRESENTATION: Eligible defendants are entitled to public defender representation[cite: 1].

Section 8. PRIVACY AND COMMUNICATIONS: Government personnel shall not misuse private community data for roleplay advantage[cite: 1].

Section 9. RIGHT TO PETITION: Residents may file complaints, appeals, and petitions without fear of retaliation[cite: 1].

Section 10. RIGHT TO VOTE: Qualified citizens have the right to participate in elections[cite: 1].

Section 11. PROTECTION FROM ARBITRARY PUNISHMENT: Government action must have a valid legal or emergency basis[cite: 1].

Section 12. RIGHT TO A FAIR HEARING: Courts and administrative bodies shall provide a neutral decision-maker and written decisions[cite: 1].

TITLE I. RULES OF COURT, DECORUM, AND TRIAL PROCEEDINGS

§ 101. Courtroom Decorum and Conduct: (a) All parties appearing before the Supreme Court or District Court of the State of Melview shall maintain professional roleplay decorum, addressing judicial officers as "Your Honor[cite: 3]." (b) Disruptive conduct, spamming, weapon brandishing, or contemptuous speech within judicial facilities shall result in summary ejection, contempt citations (§ 8-409), and immediate detention by the Court Bailiff[cite: 3].

§ 102. Trial Procedure and Plea Bargaining: (a) Criminal trials shall proceed in four distinct phases: (1) Arraignment & Plea; (2) Presentation of Evidence & Witnesses; (3) Closing Arguments; and (4) Verdict & Sentencing[cite: 3].

TITLE II. STATE BAR EXAMINATION AND ATTORNEY LICENSING

§ 201. Admission to the State Bar: Candidates must achieve a passing score of at least 80% on the Melview Uniform Bar Examination[cite: 3].

TITLE III. SEARCH & ARREST WARRANT STANDARDS

§ 301. Probable Cause Affidavits: Peace officers must submit a written sworn affidavit establishing probable cause before a search or arrest warrant may issue[cite: 3]. General warrants are strictly prohibited[cite: 3].

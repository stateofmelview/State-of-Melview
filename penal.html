MCR-2026-01: Standard Law Enforcement & Pursuit Operating Rules

MANDATORY PROTOCOL
§ 401. Pursuit Intervention Technique (PIT) Protocol:

Peace officers are strictly prohibited from executing PIT maneuvers at speeds exceeding 35 MPH unless lethal physical force has been formally authorized by field command or the target vehicle poses an active deadly threat to pedestrians.

§ 402. Custodial Duration Limit:

Pursuant to Title 8 § 103, no citizen may remain in administrative or penal custody exceeding 30 minutes per arrest event across all stacked offenses combined.

§ 403. Warrant Execution & Particularity:

All property searches and premises warrants require a judicial order sworn with particularity under Article I, Section 6 of the Constitution of Melview.
